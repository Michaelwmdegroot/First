PLAYABLE CONCEPT // V0.2

EUROPE IS OCCUPIED.

A militant cult known as The Order controls the roads, farms and towns. Civilians are forced into labour camps. Disobedience is punished by death.

You found an old emergency shelter buried beneath the countryside. It is cramped, but hidden and safe. A cot, two days of food and a little drinking water are all that remain. If you want a future here, you will have to leave safety, scavenge the occupied world, and dig outward through the earth.
