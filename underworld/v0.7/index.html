PLAYABLE CONCEPT // V0.7

EUROPE IS OCCUPIED.

A militant cult known as The Order controls the roads, farms and towns. Civilians are forced into labour camps. Disobedience is punished by death.

You found an old emergency shelter hidden beneath a small city park. Above you, The Order controls the streets. Apartment blocks, abandoned cars and shuttered shops surround the park. The shelter is safe, but its supplies are almost gone. To survive, you must scavenge the city and slowly carve a larger home from the earth.
